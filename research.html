Indian Contract Act, 1872

Section 27 — Agreement in Restraint of Trade Void

"Every agreement by which any one is restrained from exercising a lawful profession, trade or business of any kind, is to that extent void."
Commercial Takeaway: Post-termination non-compete clauses cannot be enforced against employees or founders in India, regardless of geographic reasonableness or duration.
Landmark Precedent: Percept D'Mark (India) (P) Ltd. v. Zaheer Khan, (2006) 4 SCC 227.

Companies Act, 2013

Section 180(1)(c) — Restrictions on Powers of Board (Borrowing Limits)

"The Board of Directors of a company shall exercise the power to borrow money, where the money to be borrowed, together with the money already borrowed by the company will exceed aggregate of its paid-up share capital, free reserves and securities premium, only with the consent of the company by a special resolution."
Commercial Takeaway: Venture debt, bank facilities, and convertible notes exceeding capital buffers must be ratified via general meeting and filed on MCA Form MGT-14.

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Section 8 — General Obligations of Data Fiduciary

"A Data Fiduciary shall implement reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breach; and in the event of a personal data breach, give the Board and each affected Data Principal intimation of such breach in such form and manner as may be prescribed."
Commercial Takeaway: Data processors operate strictly on Data Fiduciary contracts. Incident notifications are statutory, not discretionary.

Arbitration & Conciliation Act, 1996

Section 7 — Arbitration Agreement

"An arbitration agreement shall be in writing. An arbitration agreement is in writing if it is contained in a document signed by the parties or in an exchange of letters or electronic communication."
Commercial Takeaway: Electronic assent in SaaS agreements is legally recognized as a written arbitration clause in India.
