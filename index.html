RAG Technology Architecture

Grounded in Legal Knowledge

How Lexora eliminates hallucination through strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation rooted in primary legal sources.

Architected for Verifiable Legal Accuracy

Lexora is designed to combine artificial intelligence with a structured legal knowledge base. Instead of relying only on an AI model's stored knowledge, the system can retrieve relevant legal provisions and supporting sources before generating a response.

"Legal intelligence cannot rely on probabilistic guesswork. Every response must trace directly to recognized statutory provisions, regulatory gazettes, or binding case precedents."

How Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Works

When a legal query is processed, Lexora converts the question into a high-dimensional vector, searches indexed legal databases, extracts verified citations, and feeds that explicit context into our specialized language model.

  • Elimination of Hallucinations: Responses are bounded strictly within retrieved factual context.
  • Point-in-Time Relevance: Reflects the most recent statutory amendments and regulatory updates.
  • Source Traceability: Direct paragraph citations allow counsel to cross-examine outputs instantly.
