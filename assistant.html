Can my company terminate this employment contract?

Employee has been with us for 14 months, agreement specifies 30 days notice or pay in lieu. Are there statutory severance mandates under Indian Law?

🧠 Lexora Preliminary Legal Memorandum

Based on the contractual context provided and codified Indian employment statutes, several key aspects of the termination must be evaluated:

1. Notice Period vs. Pay in Lieu (Contractual Clause)

If the contract stipulates 30 days' written notice or salary in lieu thereof, exercising termination for convenience requires paying gross salary (basic + allowances) for the 30-day period immediately on or before the effective relief date.

2. Applicability of Industrial Disputes Act, 1947

If the employee falls under the definition of a "workman" (non-managerial / non-supervisory capacity earning below statutory thresholds), Section 25F mandates: (a) 1 month's notice in writing or wages in lieu; and (b) Retrenchment compensation equivalent to 15 days' average pay for every completed year of continuous service.

3. State Shops and Commercial Establishments Act

Local state legislation (e.g. Karnataka, Maharashtra, Delhi) governs severance rules. In Delhi, Section 30 requires 1 month's notice or wages for employees with more than 3 months of service, except in cases of proven misconduct after inquiry.

Next Steps for Company: Prepare a formal termination notice citing the contractual convenience clause, calculate full and final settlement (including accrued unavailed leave and pro-rata bonus), and require execution of a standard release and non-disparagement acknowledgement.

Sources: Indian Contract Act 1872 Sec 73 Industrial Disputes Act 1947 Sec 25F Delhi Shops & Establishments Act Sec 30
