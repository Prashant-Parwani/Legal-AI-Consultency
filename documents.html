11.2 Termination for Convenience: High Attention

"Customer may terminate this Agreement at any time without cause upon thirty (30) days written notice. Supplier shall immediately refund all unearned prepaid fees."

▲ Risk: Asymmetrical termination rights; no reciprocal exit right or break-fee for Supplier.

14.2 Indemnification & Liability: Medium Attention

"Supplier shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer without limitation against any third-party claims, consequential damages, lost profits, and incidental operational losses..."

▲ Risk: Uncapped consequential liability; conflicts with Section 73 of Indian Contract Act 1872.

18.1 Data Privacy & Security: Review

"Parties shall safeguard proprietary information and comply with Indian data regulations during the term."

ℹ Compliance: Requires explicit Data Fiduciary / Processor clauses as mandated under Section 8 of DPDP Act 2023.

Recommended Commercial Redlines
Clause 14.2 — Liability Cap Redline
"Supplier's aggregate liability under Section 14.2 shall be strictly capped at total fees paid during preceding 12 months, excluding speculative or consequential damages."

Protects enterprise against open-ended liability. Aligns with standard Indian B2B SaaS norms.

Clause 11.2 — Reciprocal Notice Exit
"Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice, subject to settlement of outstanding invoices."

Establishes bilateral exit reciprocity and prevents stranded infrastructure costs.
